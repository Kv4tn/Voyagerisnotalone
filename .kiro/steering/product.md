# Product Steering: Voyager Is Not Alone

## Product Vision

**Voyager Is Not Alone** is a narrative-driven 2D pixel-art space exploration RPG that combines character-driven storytelling, crew relationship management, aerospace engineering simulation, and logical puzzle solving. Players control a named astronaut protagonist exploring Asterion space station, building relationships with four crew members, commanding spacecraft Voyager, and making consequential decisions that lead to multiple endings.

## Target Audience

- Indie game enthusiasts who enjoy narrative-driven experiences
- Fans of FTL: Faster Than Light, Space Haven, and story-rich RPGs
- Players interested in space exploration and science fiction
- Gamers who appreciate pixel art aesthetics
- Players who enjoy relationship-building and consequential choices

## Core Experience

The game delivers:
- **Exploration**: Freely explore interconnected 2D environments (space station, spacecraft, planet)
- **Relationships**: Build trust and bonds with four distinct crew members through dialogue and decisions
- **Engineering**: Manage spacecraft systems with realistic physics (Tsiolkovsky equation, power management)
- **Puzzles**: Solve logical challenges (power grids, signal decoding, star navigation, investigations)
- **Narrative**: Experience branching storylines with multiple endings based on player choices
- **Discovery**: Uncover mysteries about an abandoned expedition and alien intelligence

## Success Criteria

### Hackathon MVP (Kiroween 2026)
- **Deliverable**: 30-45 minute polished prologue
- **Success Metrics**:
  - Playable from start to prologue conclusion
  - All core systems functional (player, NPC, dialogue, quests, relationships, puzzles)
  - Two station floors fully explorable
  - At least 3 NPCs with branching dialogue
  - 2 integrated puzzles (power grid, signal analysis)
  - One quest chain demonstrating system integration
  - Save/load working
  - Windows and macOS builds

### Full Game Release
- **Deliverable**: 8-10 hour complete experience across 7 chapters
- **Success Metrics**:
  - Five distinct endings with character epilogues
  - 20+ quests (main story and side quests)
  - Full relationship system with meaningful consequences
  - 5+ puzzle types with multiple instances
  - Complete station (5 floors), spacecraft interior, and planetary locations
  - 15+ NPCs with unique personalities and stories
  - Replayability with different choices leading to different outcomes

## Design Principles

1. **Meaningful Choices**: Every decision should have consequences, not just dialogue flavor
2. **Integrated Systems**: Engineering, relationships, puzzles, and narrative must influence each other
3. **Data-Driven Content**: Separate content (dialogue, quests) from engine code for easy expansion
4. **Atmosphere**: Maintain sense of isolation, mystery, and wonder through visuals and sound
5. **Accessibility**: Clear UI, readable text, intuitive controls, optional tutorials
6. **Performance**: Maintain 60 FPS even on modest hardware
7. **Modularity**: Clean architecture enabling parallel two-developer workflow

## Out of Scope

- Multiplayer or co-op gameplay
- Procedural generation (handcrafted content only)
- Real-time combat or action mechanics
- Voice acting (text-based dialogue)
- Mobile or console ports (desktop only for MVP)
- Extensive customization of protagonist appearance (name only for MVP)

## Release Strategy

### Phase 1: Hackathon MVP (December 2026)
- Submit to Kiroween 2026 Frankenstein category
- Gather feedback from judges and community
- Identify polish areas and bug fixes

### Phase 2: Full Development (Q1-Q2 2027)
- Implement remaining 5 chapters
- Expand NPC roster and dialogue trees
- Create additional puzzles and quests
- Build planetary exploration sections
- Implement all five endings

### Phase 3: Polish and Release (Q3 2027)
- Comprehensive playtesting
- Balance tuning
- Localization (Russian, Kazakh)
- Marketing materials and trailer
- Steam/itch.io release

## Metrics and Feedback

### Development Metrics
- Lines of code written
- Systems implemented vs planned
- Test coverage percentage
- Performance benchmarks (FPS, memory, load times)

### Player Metrics (post-release)
- Average playtime
- Completion rate
- Endings distribution
- Most/least popular NPCs and quests
- Player feedback surveys
- Bug reports and crash analytics

## Stakeholders

- **Lead Developer (Dev 1)**: Systems architecture, core gameplay, technical implementation
- **Content Developer (Dev 2 / Dias)**: Environments, NPCs, dialogue, quests, art assets
- **Hackathon Judges**: Primary audience for MVP
- **Players**: Ultimate audience for full game
